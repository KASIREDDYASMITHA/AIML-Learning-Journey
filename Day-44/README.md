
# Day 44 - Introduction to Deep Learning

## Overview
Today, I learned the fundamentals of Deep Learning, the biological neuron, artificial neurons, perceptrons, weights, bias, activation functions, and the development of modern Deep Learning.

## Topics Covered
- History of Artificial Intelligence and AI winters
- Limitations of traditional Machine Learning
- Feature engineering
- Biological neurons and their working
- Artificial neurons and perceptrons
- Inputs, weights, weighted sum, and bias
- Activation functions: Step, Sigmoid, and ReLU
- Limitations of a single perceptron
- Neural networks and multiple layers
- Importance of data, GPUs, and training algorithms
- Backpropagation and improvements in deep learning
- AlexNet and the rise of Deep Learning
- Applications and milestones of Deep Learning
